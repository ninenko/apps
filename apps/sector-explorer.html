Sector explorer

Choose a country. Move the year (slide, press Play, or touch the chart). See how the sectors change.

Primary = farming, fishing, mining, oil and gasSecondary = industryTertiary = services

Careful: the World Bank puts mining, oil and gas inside "industry". So in the charts they are in the secondary part. In our course, mining and oil are primary. The company examples below follow our course.

Explore.
  1. Look at the primary sector. Is its share of jobs bigger or smaller than its share of GDP? What does that tell you?
  2. Change the year. Which sector gets bigger over time? Which gets smaller?
  3. Compare Country A with Country B. What is the biggest difference?
Data: World Bank, World Development Indicators (downloaded on 1 October 2026 and saved inside this page, so it also works without the internet). Share of GDP: NV.AGR.TOTL.ZS, NV.IND.TOTL.ZS, NV.SRV.TOTL.ZS. Share of jobs (modelled ILO estimates): SL.AGR.EMPL.ZS, SL.IND.EMPL.ZS, SL.SRV.EMPL.ZS. Industry also includes mining, electricity and construction. Grey "Other" is taxes on products, which the World Bank does not put in a sector. Companies are examples: many companies work in more than one sector, and we put each one where its main work is. "No number" means the World Bank has no number for that year.
